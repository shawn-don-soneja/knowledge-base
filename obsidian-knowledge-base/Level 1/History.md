> "Those who cannot remember the past are condemned to repeat it." — George Santayana

### Ancient History (c. 3400 BCE – 476 CE)

Rise of the first civilizations and classical empires.

- **Mesopotamia** — Sumer, Babylon, Akkad, Assyria — cuneiform, Code of Hammurabi
- **Egypt** — Old, Middle, New Kingdoms — pharaohs, pyramids, hieroglyphs
- **Indus Valley** — Harappa, Mohenjo-Daro — advanced urban planning
- **China** — Shang, Zhou, Qin, Han — oracle bones, Great Wall, Silk Road
- **Greece** — Athens (democracy), Sparta (oligarchy), Alexander the Great
- **Rome** — Republic → Empire → Fall (476 CE) — Roman law, aqueducts, Pax Romana
- **Persia** — Achaemenid dynasty, Cyrus the Great, Royal Road
- **India** — Maurya (Ashoka, Buddhism), Gupta (Golden Age)

### Postclassical / Medieval (c. 476 – 1450)

Spread of major religions, trade networks, and feudal systems.

- **Byzantine Empire** — Eastern Rome, Justinian's Code, Orthodox Christianity
- **Islam** — Rise (610 CE), Umayyad & Abbasid caliphates, Islamic Golden Age
- **China** — Tang, Song, Yuan (Mongols), Ming — paper, compass, gunpowder
- **Europe** — Feudalism, Crusades, Magna Carta (1215), Black Death (1347-1351)
- **Africa** — Ghana, Mali (Mansa Musa), Songhai empires; Swahili city-states
- **Americas** — Maya, Aztec, Inca — calendars, pyramids, quipu
- **India** — Delhi Sultanate, Chola Empire, spread of Islam

### Early Modern (c. 1450 – 1750)

Global exploration, religious upheaval, and the birth of modern science.

- **Renaissance** (14th–17th c.) — Italy, humanism, da Vinci, Michelangelo, Gutenberg press
- **Reformation** (1517) — Luther, Calvin, Church of England, Thirty Years' War
- **Age of Exploration** — Columbus (1492), Vasco da Gama, Magellan — European colonization begins
- **Scientific Revolution** — Copernicus, Galileo, Newton — heliocentrism, scientific method
- **Enlightenment** — Locke, Rousseau, Voltaire — reason, rights, social contract

### Modern (c. 1750 – present)

Industrialization, revolutions, global wars, and the contemporary world.

- **Industrial Revolution** (late 1700s) — Steam engine, factories, capitalism, urbanization
- **American Revolution** (1776) — Declaration of Independence, Constitution
- **French Revolution** (1789) — Liberty, Equality, Fraternity — Napoleon
- **Nationalism** — Unification of Italy (1861) and Germany (1871)
- **World War I** (1914–1918) — Trench warfare, League of Nations
- **World War II** (1939–1945) — Total war, Holocaust, atomic bomb, UN
- **Cold War** (1947–1991) — US vs. USSR, nuclear arms race, decolonization
- **Post-Cold War** — Globalization, digital revolution, climate change

### American History (colonial – present)

> Key documents: Declaration of Independence (1776), Constitution (1787), Bill of Rights (1791), Emancipation Proclamation (1863), 19th Amendment (1920)

- **Colonial** (1492–1763) — 13 colonies, slavery, French & Indian War
- **Revolution** (1765–1789) — Independence, Revolutionary War, founding
- **Early Republic** (1789–1823) — Washington, Jefferson, Louisiana Purchase, War of 1812
- **Expansion** (1820s–1850s) — Manifest Destiny, Mexican-American War, abolitionism
- **Civil War** (1861–1877) — Lincoln, Emancipation, Reconstruction, 13th/14th/15th Amendments
- **Gilded Age** (1870s–1920) — Industrialization, immigration, trusts, labor, women's suffrage
- **Depression & WWII** (1929–1945) — New Deal, Pearl Harbor, US as superpower
- **Cold War** (1945–1990s) — Korea, Vietnam, Civil Rights Act, Space Race
- **Modern** — 9/11, War on Terror, Great Recession

### European History

- **Ancient** — Greece, Rome, Christianity
- **Medieval** — Feudalism, Crusades, Magna Carta, Black Death, Hundred Years' War
- **Renaissance & Reformation** — Italy, humanism, Luther's 95 Theses, printing press
- **Revolution & Empire** — French Revolution, Napoleon, Industrial Revolution, nationalism
- **World Wars** — WWI (1914–1918), WWII (1939–1945), Holocaust, Cold War division
- **Modern** — EU, Maastricht Treaty, euro, Brexit
