`Philosophy` is the action of reasoning. It explores a lot of concepts *related* to the human experience. It's a bit of an intangible world. But it helps us to define what we *should* do.

It has to do with thinking, and understanding. 
### Types
**metaphysics** (reality), "thinking about thinking"
**epistemology** (knowledge), 
**ethics** (morality) - Moral Philosophy 
**aesthetics** (beauty), 
and **logic** (reasoning)
