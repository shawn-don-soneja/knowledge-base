> `Creativity` is the act of expressing the self.
> 
> We are all humans - homo sapiens to exact. We are animals first, with physical needs, social needs, and uniquely emotions and "intelligence".
> 
> When we talk creativity, it's be able to tap into this existence, and to "create". Regardless of whether you know what you're doing or not.
> 
> Creativity can show up in many ways. Below are just a few core ones in my world:

- [[Music]] - creativity in instruments and sound
- [[Art]] - creativity through visuals
