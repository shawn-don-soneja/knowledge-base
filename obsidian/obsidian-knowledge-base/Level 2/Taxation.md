
> Mandatory payment imposed by governmental organizations
> to provide public goods and services for the benefit of the community as a whole.

### Types
	
1. Taxes on what you **EARN**
	1. Income Tax
			Income taxes are federal, state, and local taxes that may be collected on income, both earned (salaries, wages, tips, commissions) and unearned (interest, dividends). Most of the federal government’s revenue, or money it receives, comes from income taxes.
2. Taxes on what you **BUY**
	1. Sales Tax
	2. Tariffs (applied when importing goods)
3. Taxes on what you **OWN**
	1. Property tax
	2. Estate Inheritance Tax
	3. Gift tax
